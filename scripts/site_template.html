Retail Cancellation Leakage Analysis

How much revenue do cancellations cost an online retailer, and how much of it can be acted on? An SQL and Python analysis of 1.07 million real transaction rows (UCI Online Retail II, Dec 2009 to Dec 2011).

Method and limits

Raw rows are loaded unmodified, cleaned in SQL with four documented rules, and each cancellation is classed as a same-day reversal when the same customer, product and opposite quantity occur within 24 hours of the sale. The site is rebuilt from the original data and published only after all tests pass. Limits: the data has no cancellation reasons, the reversal rule is a heuristic, and figures are list-price revenue, not margin.

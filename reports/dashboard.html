Retail cancellation leakage

UCI Online Retail II, one UK giftware retailer, Dec 2009 - Dec 2011. Product lines only; see README for cleaning rules.

£19.7mGross product sales (Dec 2009 - Dec 2011)
3.65%Headline cancellation rate (by value)
50%Of cancelled value is same-day reversals
1.88%Adjusted rate after removing reversals

Adjusted rate = (non-reversal cancellations) / (gross sales minus reversed sales). A reversal is a cancellation of the same customer, SKU and quantity within 24 hours of the sale. Cancellation reasons are not in the data, so cancellations include returns, order-entry corrections and cancelled orders.
